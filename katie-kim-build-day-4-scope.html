1. Meeting-Review Workflow — Auto-Assign, Duplicate Check & Flagging
Test and refine the automations that turn transcripts into reviewed tasks
🔴 High Priority ▶
What's already there: Fathom transcripts land in the Call Transcripts table via webhook (long calls are split automatically), and tasks are already being auto-created and flagged Pending Approval. This build day refines that flow so the tasks land matched, assigned and de-duplicated.
From the call: "I want to be able to go into call transcripts and just review the ones I haven't reviewed yet… I don't want to review a meeting without having that duplicate task check."
Match the meeting to the right records
  • Auto-populate Related Project, Related Company and Meeting Attendees from the meeting title
    Search order: project → company → contact (project first — it appears in the title most often). On a clean single match, populate the related fields. On any ambiguity (e.g. two "Mike"s, two companies), populate nothing — never write the wrong data.
    Automation
  • "Call Transcripts for Review" interface
    A view that isolates meetings Katie hasn't reviewed yet, and a Reviewed state once she's confirmed the project, company and contacts. Lets her crank through transcripts when she has time, then move each from Pending to Reviewed.
    Interface
Assign & de-duplicate (button-triggered, after Katie confirms)
  • Auto-assign tasks to the people associated with the project
    Once Katie confirms the company, project and contacts, a button runs the assignment: each task in the meeting is assigned to the relevant contact / team member for that project.
    Automation
  • Duplicate check — two levels
    Level 1: flag duplicate tasks generated inside the same meeting. Level 2: a button compares the meeting's tasks against all existing tasks on that project (from prior meetings) and flags likely duplicates — e.g. "submit for permit" vs "did you submit to the city". De-dup is at project level.
    AutomationAI
  • Show the suspected duplicate's title and a link to the original
    Because the check runs from the transcript view, the earlier duplicate lives in a different view. The flag will carry the original task's title and a link to the record so Katie can decide which to keep or delete without hunting for it.
    Automation
Decision: Tasks stay Pending Approval until the meeting is reviewed — it doubles as Katie's "review this meeting" signal. Notifications only fire after approval (see Section 2).

2. Contact & Assignee Linking — Internal / External Assignment & Notifications
One assignment field that works for the team now and clients later
🔴 High Priority ▶
The constraint: The Assignee field only accepts paid Airtable users, so tasks can't be assigned directly to external contacts. Adding everyone as a paid seat isn't the answer.
  • Assign tasks to Contacts (internal and external)
    All task assignment runs through the Contact field, so anyone — team or client — can be linked to a task without paying for a seat. Sets up the future where clients see their own tasks in a portal.
    Field
  • Link Contact → User so the team still gets native notifications
    When a contact is also an internal team member, the automation auto-populates the Assignee (User) field — keeping the Airtable bell notification and the "current user" My Tasks view working. External contacts simply leave Assignee empty.
    AutomationField
  • Notifications fire only after a meeting is approved
    No one is notified when tasks are first auto-created (they're unverified). Once the meeting is reviewed, de-duplicated and approved, assignees are notified that their tasks are ready.
    Automation
Katie owns: Adding all team members into the Contacts table so they can be linked as assignees. Katie

3. Company & Contact Data Structure
Make sure the matching automation has clean fields to land in
🔴 High Priority ▶
  • Rename "Related Company" → "Related Companies"
    This field holds the project's client company — the one the project is actually for (e.g. Highmark Distillery for the Highmark project). The plural label reads correctly for the team.
    Field
  • Add a separate "Related Vendors" connected field
    Still links to the Companies table, but kept distinct from the client company — for engineers and vendors on a project (e.g. Southwest Engineers, Thoroughbred Spirits). Keeps "who the project is for" separate from "who's working on it".
    FieldTable
  • Solo-person-as-company rule
    When a project's client is an individual with no business, they come in as a Contact and are also used as the company (e.g. Jennifer Humphrey = contact and company for the Humphrey project).
    Table
Watch-out from the call: The same company can have different service lines that are really separate projects (Highmark Distillery vs Highmark real-estate vs a vendor like Thoroughbred Spirits). Consistent naming in calendar titles and company records is what keeps the matching accurate.

Set up the fields the status-report layer will use
🔴 High Priority ▶
  • Add a "Status Report" attachment field on Call Transcripts
    Behaves like the existing contract field — holds the generated meeting-notes PDF against the meeting record. Becomes the source the future portal can link to.
    Field
  • Add a "Notes Reviewed" approval field
    So nothing AI-generated reaches a client unreviewed — Katie approves the report before it's shared with the client or team.
    Field
Katie owns the status-report generation. Claude builds each meeting report on Katie's side — summary from the transcript, tasks pulled from the reviewed Airtable records (not the raw transcript) — and writes the PDF back into the Status Report field. Because Airtable can't trigger Claude directly, this runs as a scheduled batch ("any reviewed meeting with no notes → generate"). It connects once the data layer is solid. Katie AI
